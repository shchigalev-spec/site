## Realism criteria

- winter and northern context is visible through snow management, low light, service roads, utilities and working-scale surroundings;
- materials show minor wear, tyre tracks, footprints and non-uniform light;
- generated images contain no baked text, prices, routes, logos, interface controls or numerical claims;
- overlays, labels and facts remain live HTML/SVG;
- unsupported documentary implications are blocked by adjacent disclosure labels.

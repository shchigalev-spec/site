# R1 general hero sequence

All four frames are Codex-generated visualizations. They use the two user-supplied winter references only for realism, atmosphere, northern scale, and image-quality direction; neither reference was copied as a layout.

Three final-state candidates were generated. Candidate 03 was selected for the clearest left-side copy field, plausible road and site scale, coherent winter geography, and the strongest documentary character. Candidates 01 and 02 remain beside the selected frame as review evidence.

Aligned state edits preserve one drone camera, horizon, road, snowbanks, footprints, lighting, and colour grade:

1. `state-01-empty`: surveyed and compacted snow-covered site.
2. `state-02-foundations`: screw piles, bearing pads, grid and staging.
3. `state-03-assembly`: partial modular groups, one crane and one delivery vehicle.
4. `state-04-operational`: completed northern modular complex.

Prompt constraints common to every frame: documentary northern Russian industrial photography, physically plausible scale, straight module geometry, sparse warm work lights, no text, UI, logos, numbers, watermarks, fantasy machinery, warped structures, overprocessed HDR, or painterly AI styling.

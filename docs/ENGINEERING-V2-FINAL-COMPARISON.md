## Strategic result

Engineering V2 is no longer a catalogue of acoustic widgets. It behaves as a diagnostic narrative: identify the symptom, trace the transmission route, locate the renovation moment, inspect the relevant construction, review bounded measured evidence, assemble a scenario, and then move to diagnosis.

The implementation deliberately does not invent price, schedule, certificate, guarantee, address, construction specification, or customer testimony. Case artwork remains labelled as illustrative, and production delivery remains blocked until the owner supplies the contact, legal, analytics, domain, and Bitrix configuration listed in `ENGINEERING-V2-CONTENT-TODO.md` and `ENGINEERING-V2-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`.

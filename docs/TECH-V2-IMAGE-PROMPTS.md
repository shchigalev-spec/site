# Tech V2 image prompts

The image generator was used for clean architectural raster plates. All signal paths, graphs, arrows, measurements, labels, and interface states are live HTML/CSS/SVG layers. No raster is evidence of a customer object.

## Locked-room continuity edits

### Hero clean plate

```text
Edit the supplied room without redesigning its architecture. Preserve camera, lens, crop, window grid, furniture, lighting direction and 16:9 composition. Remove acoustic ribbons, waveforms, glow trails, labels, measurements and UI. Reconstruct hidden surfaces as a clean premium dark apartment at dusk. No text, logo, people, infographic, arrows or fake interface.
```

### Hero cutaway plate

```text
Edit the clean plate into a restrained architectural cutaway of the exact same room. Lock camera, lens, crop, perspective, window grid, furniture and every architectural edge. Reveal construction only along the principal wall, ceiling edge and floor edge. Keep most of the finished room visible for pixel-consistent crossfade. No text, waveform, arrows, labels, measurements, UI or people.
```

### Renovation continuity

```text
Edit the supplied unfinished room into new-build, orderly mid-renovation and finished states of the same room. Preserve camera, perspective, openings, columns, doorway, service routes, light direction and architectural edges. Expose real junctions during construction and use restrained warm materials when finished. No embedded text, glow, waveform, arrows, measurements or UI.
```

## Regenerated diagnostic plates

### Apartment path laboratory

```text
Clean oblique architectural apartment plate in the Tech graphite-and-warm-material world, with readable room boundaries, wall/ceiling/floor junctions and ventilation route, broad uncluttered surfaces for live diagnostic overlays, realistic perspective and restrained dusk light. No path line, waveform, arrows, chart, label, number, measurement, device display, logo or fake UI.
```

### Quality-control plate

```text
Premium architectural worksite detail after acoustic construction, orderly finished junctions and accessible inspection points, restrained neutral light, one dominant object and quiet negative space for live checklist copy. No worker portrait, certificate, badge, text, arrow, chart, measurement, logo or interface.
```

### Wall, ceiling and floor service plates

```text
Generate a clean residential architectural plate dedicated to the requested surface, with credible junction geometry and enough negative space for a live coded construction diagram. Keep materials restrained and realistic. Do not embed layers, dimensions, arrows, paths, waveforms, labels, numbers, charts, device readouts, logos or fake UI.
```

## Rejected content rule

Any candidate with embedded graph, path, waveform, arrow, measurement, label, number or fake interface is rejected. The earlier `64 dB → 43 dB` candidate with a baked graph was replaced by a clean case background.

Exact internal generation IDs and seeds were not available and are not invented here. `scripts/process-tech-v2-assets.mjs` records the deterministic resize and format pipeline applied after generation.

## Result

The visible change is not “more animation.” The page now carries one diagnostic story:

`symptom → uncertainty → measurement → candidate routes → selected route → construction response → controlled result`.

The final local prototype passes the Slice 09 visual, motion, browser, conversion and performance gates. It remains blocked from production lead collection by owner-supplied domain, contact, legal, Metrica and real Bitrix data.
